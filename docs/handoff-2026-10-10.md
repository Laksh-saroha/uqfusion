## 1. What was asked, and what was done

Asked (2026-10-09/10): find out whether the corruption pipeline is wrong and fix it; use better
libraries if needed; parallelise cache building and corruption on every core; add batched inference;
re-apply the IR min-max stretch after IR fog with sensor noise restored; give glare an auto-exposure
response and calibrate AE, sun and lamp from data; audit the whole chain in physical order (items a–f);
re-score pohang04 under the new corruptions and disclose it; look for other problems; fix everything
downstream; write every finding into the README and this handoff.

Done:

1. **v1 is broken at the source** (albumentations 2.0.8): fog is a fixed 21-px blur, low light is an
   additive clip, the sun lands in the letterbox pad on 45% of frames, every filter rewrites the pad,
   and IR got visible-light filters with colour noise. README §1.
2. **Corruption v2** (`src/uqfusion/eval/corruptions_v2.py`, revision 3, code `86ce9080b2b6`): the camera
   and dataset chain in physical order, atmosphere → optics → sensor noise → AE / IR re-stretch →
   quantise, content rows only, per-frame seeding as v1. Fog is Koschmieder on per-frame metric depth
   (Depth Anything V2-Small fitted to the AHRS flat-sea range). Low light is exposure scaling plus the
   measured night noise. Glare is a sun or lamp with a fitted r⁻³ tail, a glint path and an AE. IR
   conditions end with the dataset's per-frame min-max stretch. README §2–§5, §7.
3. **Calibrated on TRAIN only**, with each constant labelled measured, fitted or assumed. The AE set point
   and metering come from 5,777 day frames (an average-metering AE). AE strength 0.375 and the sun's
   intensity and spread come from the one real sun-entry event (pohang00 L 18884–19000). The lamp's tail
   comes from 101 real night lights, and noise from a measured noise-level function. README §4.
4. **Two revisions before any v2 detection was scored.** Revision 2 fixed the IR stretch, noise
   restoration after fog and blur, the AE and the channel noise. Revision 3 fixed the glare tail: the
   Lorentzian lamp lifted the dark floor of every night frame (p05 16 against real 7), which would have
   switched off the night veto under glare for a reason absent from real frames. It was found by
   comparing the gate's own statistics with real night lights, not by any score. README §3; exposure
   ledger.
5. **Audit a–g**, README §6. The new item g ("does a corruption look real *to the gate*?") found the
   revision-2 lamp defect, and found that the veto's `veil` axis was a detector of v1's blur.
6. **Speed** (README §9). One corruption pass feeds all five checkpoints, frames are corrupted in a
   process pool, gate statistics are computed in the same pass, inference runs at batch 4 on pohang04
   (bit-identical at B = 1, |Δ AP| ~1e-5 at B = 4), scoring uses a process pool, and the pohang04
   bootstrap is memory-mapped and chunked. During the pohang04 build the GPU and CPU are both at 100%.
7. **Everything downstream re-measured** (§2 below), and the paper, venue drafts, figures and TMLR PDF
   updated. Beyond the tables the paper reports on v2, five further checks ran on development data,
   each logged in the exposure ledger before it ran:
   * the cost of the weak-IR fallback;
   * the IR merge veto and the support bonus under degraded IR;
   * the veil repair re-priced;
   * Stage 1 under v2;
   * a candidate fallback repair.
8. **The pohang04 bootstrap moved to the GPU.** The CPU path was projected at ~8 h; a bit-identical
   GPU path (`--boot-device cuda --reuse-pre`) finished it in ~40 min.

## 3. Degraded IR: the weak-IR fallback and the support bonus (new, priced on development data)

Two descriptive runs at one corruption draw (VIS 941, IR 951), five systems, ship AP, logged in the
exposure ledger before they ran. README §8.6–8.7; `docs/eval/corruption_v2/fallback_cost.md`,
`ir_merge_veto.md`; scripts `v2_fallback_cost.py`, `v2_ir_merge_veto.py`. Four new IR caches:
`runs/cache_p3dev_v2/seed*/draw941_951/gauss_ir_paired_{fog_s1,fog_s3,noise_s2,noise_s3}.pkl`.

* **The fallback costs AP.** With low-light VIS (0.1712 by day), fused − max(VIS, IR) is −0.0067 to
  −0.0125 with IR fog or noise at s1/s2. At s3 it is **−0.1135** under IR fog (veto 62%) and
  **−0.1643** under IR noise (veto 96%). Every interval is below zero, so every such cell fails the
  Table 3b claim.
* **The union loses to a near-dead IR even without a veto.** Clean VIS + IR fog s3 is −0.0096
  [−0.0128, −0.0063] against VIS alone. The mechanism is the cross-modal support bonus: switching it off
  gains +0.0113 there (+0.0050 at fog s2), while it is worth +0.0068 with clean IR and +0.0157 with
  IR noise s2. Junk IR boxes vouch for VIS false positives.
* **The IR merge veto is not the repair.** It was off in `crossmodal26m` after v1 measured it
  net-harmful. Under v2 it costs −0.0015 to −0.0169 wherever it fires, so the v1 decision stands.
* **At night,** IR noise takes IR to 0.0000 and the veto still drops VIS (0.24–0.25), so fused is 0.
  This is the §6.6 night defect, amplified.

* **A candidate repair, priced** (`fallback_fix.md`): drop `veil` from the fallback (with clean IR the
  axis is inert under v2, `veil_reprice.md`). On low-light days it gains +0.0074 to +0.0182 at s1/s2,
  turning three failing cells into passes, and +0.0942 / +0.1431 at s3, which still fail by ~0.02.
  It moves the other 31 of 36 cells by exactly 0, including fogged VIS at night with damaged IR.

Paper: §6.7 (three paragraphs), contribution 2, §8 and §9 item 25.

## 5. Decisions for Laksh

1. **Committed and pushed** 2026-10-10 as the sole author (three commits, plus the three from 10-09);
   the branch matches origin. `local.md` and `docs/status-report-2026-09-27.pdf` stay untracked.
2. **The degraded-IR holes** (§3): the weak-IR fallback (up to −0.16 AP on low-light days) and the
   support bonus (−0.0113 under IR fog s3). Each fix is a new rule, so each needs its own registration
   and development-only pricing. Candidates:
   * for the fallback, **drop `veil` from it**. This is the leading candidate: priced descriptively on
     one draw, it recovers +0.0074 to +0.1431 on low-light days and costs nothing measured elsewhere
     (`fallback_fix.md`). Register it with all four draws before adopting;
   * for support, apply the bonus only while IR passes its health bound.

   The paper discloses both (§6.7, §9 item 25). Note that development is now the only data left to
   price them on.
3. **Glare intensity.** It is fitted to one real sun event and 101 night lights; the glare/night
   verdict flips at twice the fitted intensity. Options: keep it and disclose (current text), or find
   more sun events outside TRAIN. Using any other split for this would spend it.
4. **R-D1 framing.** The paper keeps the registered v1 verdict (POSITIVE 3/4) and reports v2 (NULL) as
   a robustness check, in the abstract, contribution 1 and §6.4. If you would rather lead with "the
   pass does not survive physically modelled corruptions", that change is to framing only.
5. **Default corruption version.** `make_corruption` still defaults to v1, so every old cache
   reproduces. Switching the default to v2 would be safe for new work, but every caller that rebuilds
   a v1 record would then need `version="v1"`.
6. **TMLR length.** The v2 material grew the PDF from 43 to 47 pages; references now start on page 23.
   The next cuts are listed in `venues/README.md` item 4.
7. **Tables still on v1** (disclosed in §9 item 25): Table 3a, Tables 4/4b/4c/5, Table L and the
   constants re-price. Re-measuring them under v2 is GPU work: rebuild `runs/cache_m` conditions for
   the rest of the corruption set.

## 7. Other problems found while doing this

* `uqfusion/eval/identity.py` decoded `git diff` as cp1252, so **every cache built from a dirty tree
  before 2026-10-10 was stamped `git_dirty: False`**. Fixed; old stamps cannot be trusted on that field.
* albumentations imports torch (~2 GB commit per worker). 28 workers plus the depth model hit the
  58.9 GB commit limit, so blur and rain units are capped at 3 workers.
* The project rule computes gate statistics under `.venv`, while v2 computes them in the build pass
  under py-3.13. The two differ by ≤ 6.3e-7 relative on float statistics. The closest of 232,128 v2
  statistics sits 1.26e-5 relative from its threshold, 20× that difference, so no decision can flip
  (`gate_threshold_proximity.txt`).
* pohang04 VIS depth falls back to the sea-plane range on 276 frames (2.2%, canal and bridge
  scenes), where a near bridge gets too much fog. Disclosed.
* The exposure ledger had no entries for the v2 sensitivity rows or v2 Table 6 (development only).
  They were added on 10-10, marked as logged after the run.
