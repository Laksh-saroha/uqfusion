## 2. Why it is a null and not a near-miss

**The one pass is not a rescue.** Lowlight passes because relaxing to 0.55 barely costs
anything there in the first place (C − A = −0.0013), not because sigma won anything back.

**The mechanism is live and does nothing to AP.** This is the new fact Stage 1 adds.
`sigma_weighted` changes the fused output on 836/836 pohang00 clean frames at 0.55 and on
753/836 at 0.85 — it is not inert in the plumbing sense — yet B − A is −0.0002 and D − C is
−0.0003. The interaction (D − C) − (B − A) lies in [−0.0002, +0.0004] in every
condition × split, with every CI spanning zero inside ±0.0015.

**Relaxing correspondence buys agreement and pays for it, a third time.** Partner rate on
pohang00 clean goes 0.05% → 10.66% from 0.85 to 0.55, and ship AP falls 0.0148. That
reproduces the published −0.0138 closely despite different detectors (yolo26m vs yolo26s)
and a different frame set (pohang00 vs fit-run clean).
