## 4. What happened today, with pointers

Full account: **`docs/night-veto-axis-closed-2026-09-04.md`**. The `runs/eval/` reports are
untracked, so that file is the durable record.

| | rule | clean-night | verdict | stopped by |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| V1 `5761e9f` | remove the veto | +0.1785 | INCONCLUSIVE | guard 2: 2 cells below `ir_only` |
| V2 `c9a215c` | `night AND (q < thr)` | +0.1785 | INCONCLUSIVE | clause (b): 5 cells below `max(VIS,IR)` |
| V3 `0890a1a` | `(night OR disarmed-night) AND (q < thr)` | +0.1785 | **VOID** | day guard: day veto 0.8% → 19.9% |

**The finding that outlives all three:** on a night-capable detector, removing the veto is
worth **+0.1785** on a clean night cell but **−0.0737** on a fogged one. The veto gates on
*darkness*; what predicts whether VIS should be dropped is *VIS health*. Those were the same
variable only because the detector was crippled at night.

**Why V3 broke the day**, measured: on day frames with a glared IR, `ir_night_raw` fires on
**19.9%** of clear days and the **authority bound** cuts it to 0.6%. V3 re-admits those calls
and asks health to confirm them. With VIS clean health blocks it; with VIS `lowlight` health
*confirms* it. The two votes stopped being independent — "VIS is unhealthy" is equally true
of a corrupted daylight frame and a real night frame.

**Also landed:**

  NLL — σ exactly 0 from two agreeing cluster members, with `_EPS = 1e-9` rendering +∞ as
  ~1e17. `gaussian_nll` returns NaN now. No published number moved.
* **Label ledger** (`a3da04d`). Append-only timeline for OQ-13. Found **8,237 label files

## 6. Traps, all paid for at least once

1. **Label hashes have three incompatible scopes.** `train` = `8ed69b5974ed`
   (`label_content_hash` over the train list); `tree` = `b92739202127`
   (`restore_night_perbox.py`'s byte-only directory walk); `all` = its own number,
   comparable to nothing published. Comparing across them manufactures a false mismatch —
   it has happened twice.
2. **The GPU interpreter is the system Python 3.13**, not `.venv` (CPU torch, ~20× slower).
   `PYTHONPATH=src` is required because `uqfusion` is not installed into it.
3. **`PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8`** or any `σ` in a `print` crashes the console on cp1252.
   Reports are fine — `write_md` writes UTF-8.
4. **Redirected stdout is block-buffered.** Use `python -u` for anything long, or a
   30-minute job looks like a hang.
5. **`fog` costs ~10.5 min per draw** and is ~70% of any cache-build wall time. Budget from
   that, not from the average.
6. **Bash heredocs mangle markdown** containing backticks; use the Write tool for patch
   scripts.
