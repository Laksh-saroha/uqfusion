## 1. What was asked, and what was done

Asked (2026-10-10/11): go over the entire system and solve the gated-fusion problem, trying new
architectures; then try drastic changes that do not retrain a backbone; look at every decision again;
parallelise and use the GPU.

Done, all registered before scoring (`docs/prereg-llf-2026-10-10.md`, `docs/prereg-llf2-2026-10-10.md`,
`docs/prereg-no-retrain-2026-10-10.md` with Amendments 1–4, each logged in the exposure ledger):

1. **Learned late fusion** (LLF, LLF-Net, LLF-2; `scripts/llf.py`, `scripts/llf_net.py`). All FAIL. A
   box-level model fitted to a noisy matching target re-ranks a detector's own boxes worse than its
   confidence; restricted to stream trust (LLF-2) it holds the claim on 52/60 cells but is still worse
   than shipped on 21 cells by day.
2. **Gate zoo** (`scripts/gate_zoo.py`): `union` (veto off) IMPROVES; `selfconf`, `calunion`, `proben`
   do not; `lex` (every VIS box above every IR box) holds the claim on 60/60 by construction but does
   not IMPROVE; `demote` (vetoed stream kept as the tail) IMPROVES but still fails the claim on 22.
3. **Deep-feature trust** (E7, `scripts/deep_gate.py`, XGBoost on the GPU): FAIL (P2).
4. **Restoration** (E2, `scripts/restore.py`): no VIS method passes the clean-night constraint; IR
   non-local means selected; gated under the shipped rule it does not IMPROVE.
5. **Checkpoint ensembles and flip TTA** (E1, E5, E8; `scripts/seed_ensemble.py`): every arm IMPROVES.
   `full_tta` (five checkpoints per stream, flip TTA, gated IR NLM, no gate) is the best system measured:
   57 cells better than shipped, 0 worse, claim fails 1, mean fused ship AP 0.1862 against 0.0894.
6. **Two structural findings.** (a) Tail theorem: boxes appended below all others cannot lower AP, so a
   veto should demote, not delete. (b) Pooling penalty: a frame-level gate mixes score scales, and
   random switching on 75% of frames scores below both pure policies
   (`docs/eval/no_retrain/switching_941.md`).
7. **Constants audit** (E3, `scripts/v2_constants_audit.py`, descriptive): every fusion constant stands (IR
   capability scale, WBF threshold, support bonus); the IR merge veto and the night-only `veil` are confirmed. The
   weak-IR fallback is wrong on v2: switching it off is better on 25 cells, worse on none, claim fails 28 → 5,
   but it is on the closed night axis. `docs/eval/corruption_v2/constants_audit.md`.
8. **End-to-end throughput** (B1, `scripts/measure_e2e_fps.py`): the shipped pipeline runs at 9.1 FPS end to
   end (109.9 ms per pair), 0.31 of the derived two-stream figure; the NumPy image statistics take 60.6 ms,
   more than both detectors (38.3 ms). `docs/eval/e2e_fps_2026-10-11.md`.

Also in this change set, from 2026-10-10 before the gate work: the two degraded-IR repairs, registered and
scored on draws 942–944. R1 (the weak-IR fallback without `veil`) is adopted as preset `crossmodal26m_r`; R2
(support bonus only from a merge-healthy IR) is rejected (README §8.11, paper §6.7).

Table 8 of the paper has every arm; `docs/eval/no_retrain/`, `docs/eval/llf/`, `docs/eval/restore/` hold
the reports.
