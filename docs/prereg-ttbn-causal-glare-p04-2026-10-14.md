Written at 9:53 PM on 10-14, before any N4c2g detection exists anywhere. Logged in
`docs/exposure-ledger-2026-09-09.md` at the same time. This is the **twelfth scoring of pohang04**.
Code: `scripts/ttbn_causal2_glare_p04.py` (new; it imports `ttbn_causal2.py`'s passes and `ttbn_glare_p04.py`'s
gate and substrate, and changes neither).

**Why.** Table 3 of the paper (held-out pohang04) has a dash in the IG-BN-causal column on the glare row.
IG-BN-causal (N4c2, `docs/prereg-ttbn-variants-2026-10-11.md`, Amendments 1–2) was registered and scored on
pohang04 (P-C2) only on the nine original cells. The glare cell was added later with IG-BN+glare's
registration (P-G, `docs/prereg-ttbn-glare-p04-2026-10-12.md`). N4c2's own gate is N4's, which fires on 1.9% of
glare/clean frames, so the causal variant with N4's gate would be `union` there by construction. The column's
glare entry needs the causal variant **with the glare term**, as IG-BN+glare and IG-BN+ens+glare carry it.
Laksh asked for it on 10-14.

## The arm: N4c2g

* **Gate:** N4g's, exactly as registered and as P-G scored it: (`q_vis < 1` OR (VIS frame mean > 117.92 AND
  VIS frame sd > 62.31)) AND IR reads day AND `ir_d2 ≤ 222.7` (`ttbn_variants.glare_term`,
  `ttbn_glare_p04.gate_g_p04`). On glare/clean it is the gate P-G scored.
* **Adaptation:** N4c2's, unchanged (`ttbn_causal2.py`). Grid: pohang04's VIS list cut into blocks of 8
  consecutive frames (N4c's per-run grid; pohang04 is one run). Each block that holds a gated frame is
  normalised with BN-mix (N = 16) of the source statistics and **the batch statistics N4 measured on the previous
  block** (its closed-loop pass). The run's first block runs at source statistics. No look-ahead.
* **Passes:** N4c2's sparse plan (`ttbn_causal2._plan`): only the output passes whose records are used and the
  recording passes they read. Its development check (`runs/novel/ttbn_variants/causal2/sparse_check.json`,
  low light, draw 942) found the sparse plan bit-identical to the full grid on all 880 kept frames.
* **Needed frames:** N4g's gate on glare/clean, per draw (`ttbn_glare_p04.needed_g("glare", d)`; IR clean only).
* **Precision:** fp16 autocast, channels_last, fused statistics. This is N4c2's registered arithmetic: every N4c2
  record, development and pohang04, is fp16 (no fp32 N4c2 exists). Glare under fp16 is covered by P-G's
  development check (N4g, glare/clean/day, draw 942: fp16 0.19543 vs fp32 0.19564, Δ −0.00021, tolerance 0.0010).
  No new precision check is run.
* **Corruption:** the plain glare caches P-G built (v2 `glare`, severity 2, corruption seed = VIS draw 941–944,
  code revision as recorded in their metadata). Each block's frames are regenerated by `ttbn._frames` with the
  same spec as P-G's N4g build.
* **Records:** `runs/holdout_p04/ttbn_causal2_glare/seed{k}/draw{v}_{v+10}/gauss_vis_paired_glare_bn16_fp16.pkl`.
* **Fusion:** E4 `union` (`crossmodal26m`, `veto=None`) of the gated VIS stream (N4c2g records where the gate
  fires, plain elsewhere) with the plain IR stream. Assembly: P-G's glare substrate and `system_context`,
  development calibration injected; nothing is fitted on pohang04.

## Test P-C2G

* **Cell:** glare/clean only (VIS v2 glare s2, IR clean, draws 941–944, five system seeds). The nine P-C2 cells
  are not re-scored. In three of them (clean/blur_s2, clean/fog_s2, fog/clean) N4g's gate adds 1–2 frames on some
  draws; P-G measured that addition at +0.0000 for N4g, and it is not built here.
* **Gate check:** in every (draw, system), the gate computed from the loaded context must equal
  `gate_g_p04("glare", None, s)` from the frame-statistics files. Otherwise nothing is reported until the
  difference is explained.
* **Statistic:** ship AP (class 0, AP50:95) against the VIS ground truth, mean over the five systems within a draw,
  then over the four draws.
* **Δ:** `union_ttbnc2g` − `union`, paired.
* **Interval:** 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the gate-free scoring's 1,000 moving-block replicates
  (`runs/holdout_p04/gatefree/boot_weights.npy`, L = 20, seed 1), as P-G and P-C2.
* **P-C2G REPLICATES** iff both hold (P-G's rule, on its one cell):
  * (a) Δ is not ≤ −0.0014 with its interval below zero;
  * (b) Δ ≥ +0.0060 with its interval above zero.
* Otherwise **P-C2G DOES NOT REPLICATE**.

**Descriptive, no verdict:**
* N4c2g − N4g (`union_ttbnc2g` − `union_ttbng`, P-G's presort), with its interval: the price of causality on glare;
* gate rate; Δ against `shipped`; the claim, fused − max(VIS, IR), with VIS the N4c2g stream.

## Prediction

N4c2g REPLICATES, with Δ close to N4g's +0.0309 and slightly smaller: N4c2 − N4 was between −0.0007 and +0.0000 on
every P-C2 cell. Expected Δ about +0.030, N4c2g − N4g between −0.0010 and 0. Glare frames are temporally
persistent (a sun glint across consecutive frames), so the previous block's statistics should be close to the
current block's, as in fog and low light.
