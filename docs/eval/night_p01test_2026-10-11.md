## Context (post hoc, descriptive; not part of the registration)

**Levels do not transfer between night episodes; the direction does.** pohang01's paired frames fall in
long contiguous chunks:
* the test split's 1,109 pairs are two stretches, frames 11,059–11,575 (517) and 26,161–26,752 (592);
* development's 1,032 night pairs are two other stretches, 7,724–8,239 and 14,395–14,910 (516 each).

Each stretch is about 52 seconds of one scene, and VIS ship AP swings 9× between stretches. Seed means
over systems:

Development rows are from systems 0–2 on draw 941's clean caches, split at frame 8,000 and frame 11,059.
The night-box filter touched train labels only, so test and development labels follow one policy.

What this means:
* **Direction transfers.** `union` above `shipped` and the claim held on both test stretches, as on
  development.
* **Levels do not.** Night AP levels are properties of the episode, not of the system.
* **The night intervals are too narrow.** The moving-block bootstrap (L = 20 frames) resamples within
  four episodes and cannot see between-episode variance. An episode-level interval would need many more
  night episodes than this dataset has.
* **The IR night flag fires on only 27.8% of these frames.** `shipped` vetoes VIS on 90.3% of them.
