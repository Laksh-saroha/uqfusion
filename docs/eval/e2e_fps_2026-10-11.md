# End-to-end two-stream throughput

500 development paired frames (every 4th), batch 1, sequential, after 50 warm-up pairs; Phase 3 seed-0 checkpoints; NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 Laptop GPU; GPU clock not pinned (samples: 1665 MHz, 51, 1665 MHz, 52, 1665 MHz, 53, 1665 MHz, 53, 1665 MHz, 54).

| stage | mean ms | median ms | p95 ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| read | 8.80 | 8.75 | 9.94 |
| vis_det | 18.88 | 18.05 | 23.37 |
| ir_det | 19.44 | 18.37 | 28.76 |
| stats | 60.60 | 60.18 | 66.50 |
| gate_wbf | 2.17 | 0.20 | 9.89 |
| llf | 4.55 | 4.30 | 7.50 |

* **Shipped two-stream pipeline, end to end: 109.9 ms per frame pair = 9.1 FPS.**
* With LLF in place of the shipped gate: 112.3 ms = 8.9 FPS.
* One detector's `predict()` on the same frames and clock (the protocol of the paper's 57.0): 17.1 ms = 58.5 FPS; halved, 29.3 FPS for two streams.
* End-to-end ÷ derived two-stream: 0.31.
