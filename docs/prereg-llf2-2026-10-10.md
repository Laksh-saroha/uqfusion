# Pre-registration: LLF-2, learned stream trust, development (2026-10-10)

Written after the LLF verdict (`docs/eval/llf/llf_2026-10-10.md`) and before any LLF-Net or LLF-2
number existed. The evaluation draws for LLF-2 (VIS 945–947, IR 955–957) were being built when this
was written; no detection on them had been scored.

## 1. What LLF showed, and the diagnosis

LLF failed both registered criteria. P1: the claim held on 33 of 60 cells (shipped 32). P2: 17 cells
were worse than shipped and 25 better. Two patterns:

* **Night.** LLF removed the shipped rule's night cost (+0.16 to +0.23 against shipped on every
  clean-VIS night cell). Its night model never saw a night frame, and LLF stays 0.009–0.024 below VIS
  alone there. With night in training (the secondary night-block analysis) it reaches +0.0232 above
  max(VIS, IR) on clean night.
* **Day.** LLF fell 0.0174 below VIS alone on clean day, and 0.0255 below shipped. The loss is the
  same without the frame-health features (0.3314) and without the cross-stream features (0.3324).
  What both ablations keep is the box-level features: rank, relative confidence, size, aspect,
  position, σ and sibling overlap. Those let the model reorder a stream's own boxes, and a model fitted
  to a noisy credit target reorders them worse than the detector's own confidence does. The earlier
  VIS re-ranker (Table L) met the same failure at λ = 1 and survived only by mixing its prediction
  with confidence.

This diagnosis is post hoc, and the redesign follows from it. That is why LLF-2 is scored on
corruption draws no system has been scored on.

## 2. LLF-2

`scripts/llf.py`, variant `trust`. It is the same pipeline as LLF (candidates, credit target,
training rows from draw 941, model class and its hyperparameters), with the feature set restricted
so that the model can only do what a gate does:

* `is_ir`, `conf` (monotone increasing), `x_sup` (monotone increasing: the strongest support from the
  other stream at IoU ≥ 0.30), `x_higher` (monotone decreasing: a more confident other-stream box
  overlaps at IoU ≥ 0.30, the duplicate signal);
* the 16 frame-health features, unconstrained.

Within a frame, two boxes of the same stream with no cross-stream overlap keep their confidence
order. The model can move a stream's boxes as a block across frames (trust), raise a box another
sensor supports, and lower a cross-stream duplicate. That is the hand-written gate's job, learned.

## 3. Evaluation

* Folds as LLF: primary leave-one-run-out (the night run is scored by a model trained without it);
  secondary night blocks.
* **Draws: VIS 945, 946, 947 with IR 955, 956, 957**, all 60 cells, five Phase 3 systems, ship AP.
  Per system the draw mean; 95% t-interval over systems (df 4). Baselines (VIS, IR, shipped) are
  recomputed on the same draws.
* **P1:** claim fused − max(VIS, IR) not failing (≤ −0.0060 with the interval below zero) on all
  60 cells. **P2:** no cell worse than shipped beyond 0.0060 with the interval below zero, and at
  least one better. Counts also at 0.0014.
* The headline is P1 and P2 together, reported whatever they say. **No pohang04 frame is read or
  scored.**

## 4. Limits

Development only, designed after LLF's result on the same frames. Fresh draws protect against
fitting the specific corruption noise of 942–944. They do not make the frames unseen, and the
redesign is one step from a seen failure. A pass here is a development result that would need new
data to confirm.
