## 3. Disclosures made tonight (all in the ledger)

  8:52 PM by mistake. The real times were 8:40 PM and 8:38 PM. They were corrected at 8:47 PM, before any
  restored-frame detection.
* **P-C2's equivalence check failed as first written.** P-C2 runs only the block passes whose output is
  used. The first check compared that sparse build with the stored full-grid records from another process.
  It failed: 39 of 880 frames differed in box count.
  * Diagnosis: a full-grid re-run in a new process differs from the stored records just as much. Within one
    process, sparse and full grid are bit-identical on all 880 frames.
  * The check now requires bit-exact agreement within one process. It passed at 10:03 PM, before any
    pohang04 detection.
* **fp16 builds are not bit-reproducible across processes** (cuDNN autotuning): about 4% of frames' box counts
  differ, and the largest confidence difference is 0.06. Within one process they are exact. N4's fp16 check
  puts the AP effect of an fp16 build at ≤ 0.0003 on development.

  no `image_path`. `ttbn_fulltta._adapt` now identifies those frames by the raw view's path at the same index.
  No P-FT number existed before the fix, and the scoring arithmetic is unchanged.
